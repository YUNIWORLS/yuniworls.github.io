## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Microphone Not Working?

1. Check that VoiceParty has microphone access: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone
2. Make sure your mic is selected in VoiceParty's settings
3. Try a different USB port or restart your Mac

### Transcription Seems Slow?

1. Close resource-heavy apps (browsers with many tabs, video editors)
2. Check that your Mac has adequate storage (under 90% full)
3. Restart the VoiceParty app from the menu bar

### Words Appear Incorrectly?

1. Speak a bit slower and clearer
2. Reduce background noise by moving closer to your mic
3. Try retraining the accuracy model from settings
